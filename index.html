Before you buy

Is this tax, legal, or investment advice?

No. These are bookkeeping and planning templates. Deposit rules, notice periods, what may be expensed or depreciated, and whether a deal is worth buying are for a qualified professional in the property’s jurisdiction — including if you are outside the US.

What software do I need?

Microsoft Excel 365 or 2021+, or a recent LibreOffice Calc. Google Sheets usually works after you upload the file; re-check drop-downs and functions such as PMT after import. Number formats ship as US dollars — change them if you work in another currency.
